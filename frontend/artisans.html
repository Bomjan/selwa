Karma Wangmo
Master Weaver · Kishuthara
Bumthang, Bhutan

A master of Kishuthara silk weaving. Her intricate patterns are inspired by Bhutanese mythology and the natural landscape of Bumthang.

Textiles Silk Natural dyes
TD
Tashi Dorji
Wood Carver · Zorig Chusum
Trashigang, Bhutan

Tashi specialises in traditional Zorig Chusum woodcraft, carving ceremonial bowls, masks, and decorative panels using techniques from Bhutan's rich artistic heritage.

Woodcraft Carving Ceremonial
PL
Pema Lhamo
Silversmith · Traditional Jewellery
Thimphu, Bhutan

Pema crafts exquisite silver jewellery using traditional Bhutanese smithing methods. Each piece is adorned with turquoise, coral, and hand-engraved spiritual motifs.

Jewellery Silver Gemstones
SC
Sonam Choden
Potter · Traditional Ceramics
Paro, Bhutan

Sonam creates hand-painted pottery using clay sourced from the Paro valley. Her distinctive style blends traditional Bhutanese motifs with a contemporary palette.

Pottery Ceramics Hand-painted
UN
Ugyen Namgay
Prayer Flag Maker · Textile Arts
Punakha, Bhutan

Ugyen is one of Bhutan's most celebrated prayer flag makers. His flags feature hand-printed mantras and are used in monasteries and homes across the country.

Textiles Prayer flags Block print
RW
Rinchen Wangchuk
Instrument Maker · Dungchen Horn
Thimphu, Bhutan

Rinchen crafts traditional Bhutanese musical instruments, particularly the Dungchen ceremonial horn. Each instrument is hand-finished and tuned using age-old methods.

Woodcraft Instruments Ceremonial
